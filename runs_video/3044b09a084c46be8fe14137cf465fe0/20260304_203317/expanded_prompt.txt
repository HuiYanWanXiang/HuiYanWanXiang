**Animation Plan: Forced Vibration Demonstration**

**Concept:** Visualize a damped harmonic oscillator (mass-spring system) subjected to an external periodic driving force. Show the transient response settling into steady-state forced oscillation.

**Total Target Duration:** 12.0 seconds.

**Layout Constraints:**
- All objects must remain within the central 80% of the frame (safe zone).
- Text and equations must be anchored to fixed frame positions (top, sides) and not drift with animations.
- The mass-spring system is centered. Labels and axes are positioned around it without overlap.
- Color code: Mass (BLUE), Spring (GRAY), Damping (RED arrow), Driving Force (YELLOW arrow).

---

**Scene Breakdown:**

**Scene 1 (0-3s): Setup the System**
*   **0-1s:** A horizontal spring (left end fixed) and a blue mass (right end) appear centered.
*   **1-2s:** Label "m" appears above the mass. A dashed equilibrium line extends through the mass's center.
*   **2-3s:** A red dashpot/damper symbol (or arrow labeled "c") appears below the spring, connected to the mass. Label appears: "Damping Coefficient c".

**Scene 2 (3-6s): Introduce Driving Force & Equation**
*   **3-4s:** A yellow arrow labeled "F(t) = F₀ cos(ωt)" appears above, pointing to the mass. It pulses.
*   **4-5s:** The equation of motion appears top-left, anchored:
    `m ẍ + c ẋ + k x = F₀ cos(ωt)`
*   **5-6s:** Text below equation appears: "Driving Frequency ω". A wave icon `~` appears next to it.

**Scene 3 (6-10s): Demonstrate the Motion**
*   **6-7s:** The mass begins to oscillate with small, irregular amplitude (transient state).
*   **7-8s:** The oscillation smooths and grows, locking into a regular, larger amplitude.
*   **8-9s:** Text "Transient → Steady State" appears at the bottom, with an arrow progressing from left to right.
*   **9-10s:** The yellow force arrow pulses in sync with the mass's peak displacement, showing resonance. A label "ω ≈ ω₀" (driving frequency near natural frequency) flashes briefly.

**Scene 4 (10-12s): Summary & Freeze**
*   **10-11s:** All motion slows. A final amplitude "A" is highlighted with a double-headed arrow next to the mass.
*   **11-12s:** Key equation for steady-state amplitude appears top-right:
    `A = F₀ / √((k-mω²)² + (cω)²)`
    The scene holds for the final second.

**Timing Notes:**
- Scene transitions use smooth fade/transform.
- Arrow pulses and label flashes are brief (0.3s).
- The core oscillation (Scene 3) lasts 4 seconds to clearly show the transition.
