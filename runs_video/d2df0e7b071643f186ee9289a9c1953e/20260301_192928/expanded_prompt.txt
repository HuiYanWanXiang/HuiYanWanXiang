**Animation Plan: Geometric Meaning of the Definite Integral**

**Concept Breakdown:**
- Visualize the definite integral as the net signed area under a curve.
- Show Riemann sum approximation with rectangles.
- Transition from finite rectangles to the limit (integral).
- Emphasize area above x-axis as positive, below as negative.

**Strict Layout Constraints:**
- All objects must remain within the central 80% of frame width and 70% of frame height.
- Text and equations must be anchored to frame edges (not drifting).
- Rectangles must not overlap axis labels or function labels.
- Area shading must be clearly distinguishable from axes.

**Scene 1: Setup Function and Area Concept (3 seconds)**
- Draw x-axis (from x=-1 to 4) and y-axis (from y=-2 to 3).
- Plot function f(x) = sin(x) + 0.5 (or similar wave crossing x-axis).
- Label axes: "x", "y", and function "f(x)".
- Highlight integration interval [a, b] with vertical lines at x=1 and x=3.5.
- Animate shading of area under curve between a and b with light color.

**Scene 2: Riemann Sum Approximation (4 seconds)**
- Divide interval [a, b] into 4 subintervals (n=4).
- Draw rectangles using right endpoints (height = f(x_i)).
- Animate rectangles appearing one by one.
- Display sum formula: Σ f(x_i) Δx (positioned top-left, anchored).
- Shade rectangles to match area color from Scene 1.
- Show numeric approximation (e.g., "≈ 2.34").

**Scene 3: Limit to Definite Integral (3 seconds)**
- Increase partitions to n=8, then n=16 (quick morph).
- Rectangles become thinner, approximating area more closely.
- Replace sum with integral symbol: ∫_a^b f(x) dx (center-top, fixed).
- Fade out rectangles, leaving shaded area under curve.
- Display "Net Signed Area = ∫_a^b f(x) dx" (bottom-center, anchored).

**Scene 4: Signed Area Explanation (2 seconds)**
- Highlight portion where f(x) > 0 (area above x-axis) in green.
- Highlight portion where f(x) < 0 (area below x-axis) in red.
- Show net calculation: green area + (- red area) = net area.
- Final text: "Integral = Net Signed Area" (center, large).

**Timing Notes:**
- Total: ~12 seconds.
- Smooth transitions between scenes.
- Keep all labels static and legible.
- Ensure axis scaling keeps entire curve visible without clipping.
