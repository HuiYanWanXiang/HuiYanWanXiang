**Animation Plan: Geometric Meaning of Derivative**

**Concept Breakdown:**
- Derivative f'(x₀) = slope of tangent line at point (x₀, f(x₀))
- Tangent line as limit of secant lines as h→0
- Visual connection between algebraic limit and geometric slope

**Layout Constraints:**
- All objects must stay within frame boundaries (8x14.5 units)
- Text/equations anchored to fixed positions, no drifting
- Coordinate axes centered with 1 unit margins on all sides
- Tangent/secant lines constrained to visible plot area
- Labels positioned with fixed offsets to prevent overlap

**Scene Structure (Total: 12 seconds):**

**Scene 1: Setup (2.0s)**
- Axes appear: x-range [-1, 3], y-range [-1, 4]
- Function f(x) = x² plotted (smooth curve)
- Point P at (1, 1) marked with dot
- Label "P(1, 1)" anchored 0.3 units right of point

**Scene 2: Secant Line Introduction (3.0s)**
- Second point Q at (2, 4) appears
- Label "Q(2, 4)" anchored 0.3 units right of Q
- Secant line PQ drawn (dashed, blue)
- Right triangle formed: horizontal Δx=1, vertical Δy=3
- Labels: "Δx = 1" below segment, "Δy = 3" right of segment
- Equation appears top-left: "Slope = Δy/Δx = 3/1 = 3"

**Scene 3: Limit Process (4.0s)**
- Q moves along curve toward P (2.0s animation)
- At Q=(1.5, 2.25): show Δx=0.5, Δy=1.25, slope=2.5
- At Q=(1.1, 1.21): show Δx=0.1, Δy=0.21, slope=2.1
- Secant lines rotate toward limiting position
- Final position: Q coincides with P (infinitesimal distance)
- Equation updates: "lim_{h→0} [f(1+h)-f(1)]/h"

**Scene 4: Tangent Line Reveal (2.0s)**
- Secant line becomes solid tangent line (red)
- Slope value settles: "f'(1) = 2"
- Tangent equation appears: "y - 1 = 2(x - 1)"
- Geometric interpretation text bottom-center: "Derivative = Slope of tangent line"

**Scene 5: Summary (1.0s)**
- All elements remain visible
- Final text overlay: "f'(x₀) = instantaneous rate of change = tangent slope at x₀"
- Fade out

**Key Objects & Positioning:**
- Axes: centered, width=6 units, height=5 units
- Function curve: f(x)=x², smooth
- Points: P fixed at (1,1), Q variable
- Lines: secant (blue dashed), tangent (red solid)
- Labels: fixed offsets from respective points
- Equations: top-left anchored at (-5, 3)
- Interpretation text: bottom-center at (0, -2.5)

**Timing Notes:**
- Smooth transitions between scenes (0.5s crossfades)
- Point movement: ease-in-out pacing
- Equation updates synchronized with visual changes
- Final hold: 0.5s before fade
