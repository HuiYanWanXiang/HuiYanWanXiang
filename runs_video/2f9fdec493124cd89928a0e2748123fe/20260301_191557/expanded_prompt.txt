**Animation Plan: Tangent Line to a Conic Section (12 seconds)**

**Concept:** Show the geometric method for finding the tangent line to a conic (using a parabola as the primary example) at a given point `P` on the curve, based on the focus-directrix property.

**Strict Layout Constraints:**
*   **Frame Boundaries:** All objects must remain within the central 80% of the frame (padding of 10% on all sides). Use a fixed coordinate grid for placement.
*   **Text/Equation Positioning:** All labels and equations must be anchored to static screen positions (e.g., top-center, bottom-left). They must not move with animated objects after being placed.
*   **Object Management:** Remove objects when no longer needed to prevent clutter. Use transforms (e.g., ReplacementTransform) instead of drifting text.
*   **Layering:** Keep the main conic curve and the point `P` on a base layer. Animate auxiliary lines and labels on top.

**Scene Breakdown:**

**Scene 1: Setup (0-3 seconds)**
*   **0-1s:** Draw the coordinate axes (static, centered).
*   **1-2s:** Draw a parabola (e.g., `y^2 = 4x`). Label it clearly as "Parabola".
*   **2-3s:** Plot and label a point `P` on the parabola. Mark the focus `F` and draw the directrix line `l`. Label both immediately. All labels remain fixed in position relative to the frame.

**Scene 2: Geometric Construction (3-9 seconds)**
*   **3-4s:** Draw the segment `PF` connecting point `P` to the focus.
*   **4-5s:** From point `P`, draw a line perpendicular to the directrix `l`. Mark the intersection point with `l` as `Q`.
*   **5-6.5s:** Animate the construction of the perpendicular bisector of segment `FQ`. Highlight that this line is the key.
*   **6.5-7.5s:** Label this bisector line as "Tangent Line at P".
*   **7.5-9s:** Briefly fade out the construction lines (`FQ`, its bisector construction marks), leaving only the parabola, point `P`, and the final tangent line clearly visible.

**Scene 3: Generalization & Conclusion (9-12 seconds)**
*   **9-10.5s:** Display a static, concise text box at the top of the screen: "Key Property: Tangent is the perpendicular bisector of `FQ`", where `Q` is the projection of `P` onto the directrix. Ensure text does not overlap main graphics.
*   **10.5-12s:** Quickly morph the parabola into a different conic (e.g., an ellipse) while keeping point `P` and the tangent line principle visually consistent (optional, if time permits), or simply display a final summary text: "Method applies to all conic sections." Fade out.
