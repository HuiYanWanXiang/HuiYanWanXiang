Animation Plan: Pythagorean Theorem Demonstration

Concept: Visual proof of a² + b² = c² using squares on triangle sides.

Scene 1: Setup (3.0 seconds)
- Frame: 16:9 aspect ratio, all content must stay within safe margins (10% from edges).
- Start with a blank screen.
- Draw a right triangle (thick lines) at center-left.
- Label vertices: A (bottom-left, right angle), B (top), C (bottom-right).
- Label sides: a (vertical side), b (horizontal side), c (hypotenuse).
- Ensure labels are static, positioned close to sides without overlapping.

Scene 2: Construct Squares (4.0 seconds)
- On side 'a', construct a square extending outward from the triangle. Animate drawing.
- On side 'b', construct a square extending outward. Animate drawing.
- On hypotenuse 'c', construct a square extending outward from the triangle (orientation may tilt). Animate drawing.
- Color squares distinctly (e.g., light blue, light green, light yellow).
- Constraint: Squares must fit within frame; scale triangle if needed. No object drift.

Scene 3: Area Relationship (3.0 seconds)
- Display equation: a² + b² = c² near top-right, static position.
- Highlight square on 'a', fade in text "Area = a²" below it.
- Highlight square on 'b', fade in text "Area = b²" below it.
- Highlight square on 'c', fade in text "Area = c²" below it.
- Ensure text boxes do not overlap squares or each other.

Scene 4: Visual Proof (2.0 seconds)
- Animate the two smaller squares (on a and b) breaking into pieces.
- Animate these pieces rearranging to perfectly fill the square on the hypotenuse.
- This demonstrates area equivalence visually.
- Keep all movement within frame center; no pieces exit view.

Total Target: ~12.0 seconds.
