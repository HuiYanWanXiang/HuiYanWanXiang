Animation Plan: Forced Vibration Demonstration

Concept: Show a damped harmonic oscillator driven by an external periodic force, illustrating resonance behavior.

Scene 1: Setup and System Definition (3.0 seconds)
- Frame center: A horizontal spring attached to a block (mass 'm') on a surface.
- Left side: Fixed wall. Right side: Block with label 'm'.
- Spring labeled 'k' (spring constant). A dashpot symbol near block labeled 'b' (damping coefficient).
- Equation appears below: `m*x''(t) + b*x'(t) + k*x(t) = F_d(t)` (Damped oscillator equation).
- All objects remain fixed in frame, no overlaps.

Scene 2: Driving Force Introduction (3.0 seconds)
- A force arrow appears above the block, pointing right/left periodically, labeled `F_d(t) = F_0 * cos(ω_d t)`.
- The arrow oscillates in size and direction. A small graph appears top-right: time vs. F_d(t), showing cosine wave.
- Equation updates: `m*x'' + b*x' + k*x = F_0 cos(ω_d t)`.
- Block remains stationary initially. Ensure graph is small and does not overlap main objects.

Scene 3: Transient and Steady-State Response (3.0 seconds)
- Block starts moving with initial messy motion (transient), then settles into steady oscillation.
- Path traces behind block show initial irregular waves becoming regular.
- Text appears: "Transient → Steady State".
- Frequency of block oscillation matches driving frequency ω_d in steady state.

Scene 4: Resonance Demonstration (3.0 seconds)
- Parameter ω_d changes (shown by ω_d value updating near force arrow).
- Animation shows amplitude of block oscillation increasing as ω_d approaches natural frequency ω_0 = sqrt(k/m).
- Peak amplitude at ω_d = ω_0, then decreases.
- Final text: "Maximum amplitude at resonance: ω_d = ω_0".
- All text and objects constrained to frame, no drifting.

Timing: Total ~12 seconds. Smooth transitions between scenes. All objects sized to avoid clipping.
