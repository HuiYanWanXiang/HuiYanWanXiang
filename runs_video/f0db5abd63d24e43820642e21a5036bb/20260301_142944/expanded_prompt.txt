**Animation Plan: Pythagorean Theorem**

**Concept Breakdown:**
- Introduce right triangle with sides a, b, c (hypotenuse)
- Show squares built on each side
- Demonstrate area equivalence: a² + b² = c²
- Use geometric transformation to visualize proof

**Scene Structure:**

**Scene 1: Triangle Introduction (3.0s)**
- Start with blank screen
- Animate drawing of right triangle (thick lines)
- Label vertices A, B, C (C at right angle)
- Label sides: a (vertical), b (horizontal), c (hypotenuse)
- Add right angle symbol at C

**Scene 2: Construct Squares (3.0s)**
- Extend side a upward, form square with side length a
- Extend side b rightward, form square with side length b
- Extend hypotenuse c, form square with side length c
- Color squares: a² (blue), b² (green), c² (red)
- Add area labels: a², b², c²

**Scene 3: Geometric Proof (4.0s)**
- Copy the original triangle (create 4 identical copies)
- Arrange triangles around square c² to form larger square
- Show that remaining area in large square equals a² + b²
- Animate triangles moving to reveal: c² = a² + b²

**Scene 4: Equation Reveal (2.0s)**
- Display equation: a² + b² = c²
- Highlight each term with corresponding square color
- Add title "Pythagorean Theorem" above equation

**Layout Constraints:**
- Keep all objects within 80% of frame width/height
- Position triangle centered left, squares right
- Equation centered at bottom 20% of screen
- Maintain consistent spacing: 0.2 units between objects
- Fix all labels in place after creation (no drifting)
- Scale elements to ensure squares don't overlap
- Use bounding boxes check for all text elements

**Timing Notes:**
- Total: 12.0 seconds
- Scene transitions: 0.5s fade
- Drawing animations: 1-2s each
- Geometric transformations: smooth 2s animations
- Final equation display: static hold 1.5s

**Key Objects:**
- Right triangle (3-4-5 proportions for clarity)
- Three colored squares (semi-transparent fill)
- Four triangle copies (light gray)
- Area labels (LaTeX)
- Equation (large LaTeX)
- Title text

**Equations:**
- Area labels: $a^2$, $b^2$, $c^2$
- Final equation: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
- All equations use fixed positions, no movement after placement
