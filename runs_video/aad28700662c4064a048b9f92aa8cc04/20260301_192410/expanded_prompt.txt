**Animation Plan: Geometric Meaning of Integration**

**Concept Breakdown:**
- Integration as area under a curve
- Connection between Riemann sums and definite integral
- Visual progression: rectangles → more rectangles → smooth area

**Total Duration:** 12 seconds (3 scenes)

---

**Scene 1: Setup & Function (0-3s)**
- **Objects:**
  - Coordinate axes (x: -1 to 5, y: -1 to 3) centered
  - Function curve: f(x) = 0.5*sin(x) + 0.5*x + 0.5 (gentle upward curve)
  - Vertical lines at x=1 and x=4 defining integration interval [1,4]
  - Label: "∫ from a to b f(x) dx" below axes
- **Actions:**
  - Axes appear (0-1s)
  - Curve draws (1-2s)
  - Vertical lines appear (2-2.5s)
  - Label appears (2.5-3s)
- **Layout Constraints:**
  - All objects fit within frame with 10% margin
  - Label positioned 1 unit below x-axis, centered horizontally

**Scene 2: Riemann Sum Approximation (3-8s)**
- **Objects:**
  - 4 rectangles under curve between x=1 and x=4 (width=0.75 each)
  - Rectangle height = function value at left endpoint
  - Area value display: "Approximation = sum of rectangle areas"
- **Actions:**
  - Rectangles appear sequentially left to right (3-5s)
  - Each rectangle fills with light blue color as it appears
  - Area value updates with each rectangle (5-6s)
  - Rectangles subdivide into 8 thinner rectangles (6-7s)
  - Area value updates: "Better approximation" (7-8s)
- **Layout Constraints:**
  - Rectangles stay within axes bounds
  - Area text positioned right of axes, aligned vertically center
  - Text remains fixed position during updates

**Scene 3: Exact Area & Conclusion (8-12s)**
- **Objects:**
  - Smooth blue fill under curve between x=1 and x=4
  - Integral symbol morphs from sigma (∑) to integral (∫)
  - Final label: "∫ from a to b f(x) dx = Exact area under curve"
- **Actions:**
  - Blue fill expands smoothly under curve (8-10s)
  - Symbol transformation (10-11s)
  - Final label appears (11-12s)
- **Layout Constraints:**
  - Fill stays precisely within curve and vertical boundaries
  - Labels remain in same position as Scene 1
  - All text horizontally centered in lower third of frame

**Key Equations/Text:**
1. "∫ from a to b f(x) dx"
2. "Approximation = sum of rectangle areas"
3. "Better approximation with more rectangles"
4. "∫ from a to b f(x) dx = Exact area under curve"

**Timing Notes:**
- Scene transitions: smooth crossfade
- Object animations: 0.5-1s each
- Pauses: 0.5s after key moments for comprehension
- Total: 3s + 5s + 4s = 12s

**Visual Guidelines:**
- Curve: black, 3px thickness
- Rectangles: light blue with 50% opacity
- Final area fill: medium blue with 70% opacity
- Text: black, sans-serif, large enough for readability
- All elements maintain fixed positions after appearing
