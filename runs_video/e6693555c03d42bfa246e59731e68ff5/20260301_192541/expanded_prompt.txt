**Animation Plan: Geometric Meaning of the Definite Integral**

**Concept Breakdown:**
- Visualize the definite integral as the signed area under a curve.
- Show Riemann sum approximation with rectangles.
- Transition from discrete sum to continuous integral.
- Emphasize connection between algebraic expression and geometric area.

**Strict Layout Constraints:**
- All objects must remain within the central 80% of the frame (10% margin on all sides).
- Text and equations must be anchored to fixed positions (no drifting).
- Rectangles must not overlap with axis labels or other text elements.
- Area shading must be clearly visible but not obscure the curve or axes.
- All coordinate axes must be centered with equal scaling.

**Scene Structure (Total: 12 seconds):**

**Scene 1: Setup and Definition (3 seconds)**
- Axes appear: x-axis [-1, 5], y-axis [-1, 3], centered.
- Function f(x) = 0.5x + 0.3sin(2x) + 1 (gentle curve).
- Curve draws from x=1 to x=4.
- Text appears at top: "Definite Integral: ∫₁⁴ f(x) dx" (fixed position, top center).
- Subtext below: "Area under the curve between x=1 and x=4" (fixed position, below main text).

**Scene 2: Riemann Sum Approximation (4 seconds)**
- Vertical dashed lines at x=1 and x=4 (boundaries).
- Divide interval [1,4] into 6 subintervals (n=6).
- Rectangles appear sequentially (left endpoints):
  - Each rectangle: width=0.5, height=f(x_i)
  - Rectangle fills with light blue (opacity=0.3)
  - Running sum appears: Σ f(x_i)Δx (right side, fixed position)
  - After all 6 rectangles: total area ≈ 7.89 (displayed)

**Scene 3: Limit Process (3 seconds)**
- Rectangles merge smoothly (n increases from 6 to 50).
- Rectangle widths shrink uniformly.
- Blue area becomes continuous shading under curve.
- Text updates: "lim_{n→∞} Σ f(x_i)Δx = ∫₁⁴ f(x) dx"
- Final area value appears: ≈ 8.02

**Scene 4: Geometric Interpretation (2 seconds)**
- Shaded area pulses gently (highlight).
- Arrow points from integral symbol to shaded area.
- Final text: "The definite integral equals the signed area between the curve and x-axis."
- All elements hold for final 0.5 seconds.

**Timing Notes:**
- Scene transitions: 0.5 second crossfade.
- Rectangle appearances: 0.3 seconds each (sequential).
- Text animations: fade in/out, no movement.
- Total: 3 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 12 seconds.

**Key Objects & Positions:**
- Axes: centered, scaled to fit 80% of frame height.
- Curve: smooth, clearly visible (2px stroke).
- Rectangles: light blue fill (#88CCFF, opacity=0.3), black borders.
- Text areas:
  - Top: 10% from top edge
  - Right side: 15% from right edge (for equations)
  - Bottom: 12% from bottom edge (for explanations)
- Area shading: blue gradient (darker at curve, lighter at axis).

**Equations:**
1. ∫₁⁴ f(x) dx
2. Riemann sum: Σ_{i=1}^n f(x_i)Δx
3. Limit: lim_{n→∞} Σ f(x_i)Δx = ∫₁⁴ f(x) dx
4. Numerical approximations: 7.89 → 8.02

**Visual Guidelines:**
- Color scheme: blue for area, black for axes/text, red for curve.
- All text in sans-serif font, large enough for clarity.
- No overlapping: equations stay in designated zones.
- Smooth animations: no sudden jumps or teleportation.
- Final frame: clean, uncluttered, with clear takeaway message.
