Animation Plan: Pythagorean Theorem Demonstration

Concept: Visual proof of a² + b² = c² using squares on triangle sides

Total Duration: 12 seconds

Layout Constraints:
- All objects must remain within frame boundaries at all times
- Text and equations must be anchored to prevent drifting
- Maintain clear spacing between elements to avoid overlaps
- Keep triangle centered with surrounding squares positioned proportionally

Scene Breakdown:

Scene 1: Triangle Setup (3 seconds)
- Right triangle appears in center (vertices labeled A, B, C)
- Sides labeled: a (vertical), b (horizontal), c (hypotenuse)
- Right angle indicator at vertex B
- Triangle dimensions: a=3 units, b=4 units, c=5 units
- All labels anchored to their respective elements

Scene 2: Square Construction (4 seconds)
- Square with side 'a' forms on vertical leg (area = a²)
- Square with side 'b' forms on horizontal leg (area = b²)
- Square with side 'c' forms on hypotenuse (area = c²)
- Squares positioned adjacent to corresponding sides without overlapping
- Area labels appear inside each square

Scene 3: Area Comparison (3 seconds)
- Squares on legs (a² and b²) visually combine
- Combined area transforms to match hypotenuse square area (c²)
- Color coding: leg squares one color, hypotenuse square another
- Transformation maintains clear boundaries

Scene 4: Equation Reveal (2 seconds)
- Equation a² + b² = c² appears below triangle
- Numerical example: 3² + 4² = 5² appears below equation
- Both equations centered and anchored to prevent movement
- Final hold with all elements visible

Timing Notes:
- Smooth transitions between scenes
- Pauses for comprehension after key steps
- Total runtime: 12 seconds (±0.5 seconds)

Visual Guidelines:
- Keep triangle size proportional to frame
- Position squares to maximize visibility
- Use contrasting colors for different elements
- Ensure all text remains legible throughout
