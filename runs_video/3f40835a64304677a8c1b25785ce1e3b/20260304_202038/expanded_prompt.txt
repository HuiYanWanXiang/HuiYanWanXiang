**Animation Plan: Forced Vibration Demonstration**

**Concept Breakdown:**
- Show a simple harmonic oscillator (mass on spring) at rest.
- Introduce an external periodic driving force.
- Demonstrate the system's response: transient then steady-state.
- Emphasize amplitude dependence on driving frequency vs. natural frequency.

**Strict Layout Constraints:**
- All objects must remain within the central 80% of frame width and height.
- Text and equations are anchored to fixed frame positions (top, sides) and do not drift.
- Mass, spring, and axis labels have fixed size ratios relative to frame height.
- Transient and steady-state phases are clearly separated in time and visual space.

**Scene Steps & Timing (Total: ~12 seconds):**

**Scene 1: Initial System (2.0s)**
- Fixed top-left title: "Forced Vibration".
- Draw a horizontal support line anchored center-top.
- Draw a vertical spring hanging from support center.
- Attach a mass block (square) to spring bottom.
- Add still equilibrium position line (dashed) below mass.
- Label: "Natural Frequency ω₀" fixed bottom-left.

**Scene 2: Introduce Driving Force (2.5s)**
- Fixed top-right label: "Driving Force: F(t) = F₀ cos(ωt)".
- Animate a horizontal arrow (left/right) acting on mass, pulsating at a set frequency.
- Add a frequency slider control below, with ω marked and adjustable (visual only).
- Show ω ≠ ω₀ initially.

**Scene 3: Transient Response (2.5s)**
- Mass begins oscillating with small, irregular amplitude.
- Add a phase label: "Transient" fixed left-middle.
- Show superposition: natural oscillation + forced response.

**Scene 4: Steady-State (3.0s)**
- Transient dies out, leaving clean, constant-amplitude oscillation.
- Change phase label to "Steady-State".
- Update amplitude display: "Amplitude A(ω)" fixed right-middle.
- Show that oscillation frequency now matches driving frequency ω.

**Scene 5: Resonance Hint (2.0s)**
- Quickly adjust frequency slider to ω = ω₀.
- Show amplitude A growing larger (visibly but within frame).
- Add text: "Maximum at Resonance ω = ω₀" fixed bottom-center.
- Freeze final frame.
